About

I'm a Senior Product Designer with 15+ years of experience building enterprise products for major fintech and retail brands, including Wells Fargo, Edward Jones, Lowe's, and Belk.

I specialize in turning design chaos into scalable systems: leading design systems from the ground up across component architecture, documentation, governance, and cross-functional teams. As a Certified Usability Analyst (CUA), I bring a research-backed perspective to how people actually use products across mobile, responsive, and enterprise experiences.

I'm equally comfortable architecting a component library, leading design teams, or shaping the product strategy around it.

Currently, I'm part of the Design Systems team at Edward Jones, where we're exploring Figma Make and AI-agent QA workflows for enterprise systems. I'm leading efforts to evolve our variables and token libraries to support both human designers and AI-driven workflows.
